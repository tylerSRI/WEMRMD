Conclusion: The 10-Year Conversion Strategy provides the highest true After-Tax value. By spreading the conversion over a decade, Warren takes optimal advantage of his decreasing tax rate schedule while moving funds into a permanent tax-free environment and avoiding capital gains drag on reinvested RMDs.

Alternative Strategy: 5-Year SPIA

This compares the Standard 5-Year Conversion against transferring the full $1,600,000 Qualified balance into a 5-Year Single Premium Immediate Annuity (SPIA). The SPIA pays out $355,800 annually, which is fully converted to the Roth IRA. This satisfies RMD requirements entirely without leaving spillover into a taxable account.

This compares the Standard 10-Year Conversion against transferring the full $1,600,000 Qualified balance into a 10-Year Single Premium Immediate Annuity (SPIA).
